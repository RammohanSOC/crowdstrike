Common questions

What is CrowdStrike Falcon?

Falcon is CrowdStrike's cloud-native endpoint security platform. A single lightweight agent on each device connects to a cloud console, and modules such as next-gen antivirus, EDR / XDR, vulnerability visibility and identity protection are enabled by license.

What is the difference between antivirus and EDR?

Traditional antivirus mainly blocks known malware. EDR also records what happens on a device, so suspicious behavior can be spotted, investigated and contained. No security product can promise to stop every attack, so we configure and tune it to give your team the best chance of detecting and responding early.

Can you replace our current antivirus or EDR with CrowdStrike?

Yes. We plan the move in stages, test on a pilot group, and agree the cutover plan with you in advance, including how the old agent is removed.

How do you keep rollouts and updates safe?

We deploy in rings (pilot, then wider groups) and configure sensor update and content update policies so changes reach your most critical systems after they have run on less critical ones. We agree these settings with you during planning.

Will the rollout disrupt my team's work?

We plan deployments in stages to keep disruption low, and we tune policies with your team so everyday work is not interrupted more than necessary.

Can we buy or renew CrowdStrike licenses through ISS?

Yes. We provide procurement and licensing services and help right-size licenses to your needs.

Do you work with other endpoint vendors too?

Yes. We also work with Sophos and Trend Micro for endpoint protection, so you can compare options before you decide.
